Fursan is a privacy-first, decentralized streak tracker and community built on the Nostr protocol. There are no accounts and no central servers: your identity is a key stored only on your device, and your progress lives on a global network of independent relays.

FEATURES

- Streak tracking: start your streak, watch your days grow, unlock ranks and keep your longest-streak record.
- Encrypted sync: streak and journal data are synced to Nostr relays encrypted with NIP-44 (v2). Only your key can read them.
- Daily journal: write down your thoughts, triggers and wins in a private, encrypted log.
- Community chat: real-time chat rooms (general, SOS, motivation, learn) with automatic name discovery, instant sending and delivery status.
- Daily check-ins: simple mind and body check-ins with helpful insights and a calm-down screen for hard moments.
- Works offline: an smart queue stores your events while you are offline and sends them automatically when you are back online.
- Daily motivation: wisdom quotes and a full activity history.

PRIVACY

- No trackers, no analytics, no advertising, no Google Play Services.
- No location permission. Nothing about you leaves your device except what you publish under your own key.
- The full source code is available under the MIT license.
