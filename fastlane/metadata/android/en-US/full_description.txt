Fursan is a privacy-first, decentralized discipline and streak tracker built on the Nostr protocol. There are no accounts and no central servers: your identity is a locally stored key pair, and your progress lives on a global network of independent relays.

FEATURES

- Streak tracking: start your protocol, track days of Sabr (patience), milestones, ranks and longest-streak records.
- End-to-end encrypted sync: streak and journal data are synced to Nostr relays encrypted with NIP-44 (v2). Only your key can read them.
- Daily journal: record your battles, thoughts and triggers in a private, encrypted log.
- Community chat: real-time decentralized chat rooms (general, SOS, motivation, fiqh) with automatic identity discovery, optimistic sending and delivery status.
- Prayer times: local offline prayer-time calculation with configurable calculation methods, GPS or manual location, and anti-cheat check-in windows.
- Daily health check: mindset and biological protocol check-ins with streak-aware insights and an emergency lock intervention.
- Offline-first: an intelligent queue stores your events while offline and publishes them automatically when you are back online.
- Motivation archive: daily wisdom quotes and activity history.

PRIVACY

- No trackers, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no Google Play Services.
- All data stays on your device (encrypted preferences) or on Nostr relays under your own key.
- The full source code is available under the MIT license.
